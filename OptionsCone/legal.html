2. Plans and prices

OptionsCone Pro is a subscription, either monthly (19.90 USD) or yearly (189 USD). Depending on where you live, sales tax or VAT is either included or added at checkout; the checkout shows the final amount in your currency before you pay. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until it is cancelled.

3. Cancellation

You can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the current period: in OptionsCone under Pro → Manage subscription, with the link Cancel subscription here at the bottom of every page, through your Link account at link.com, or by email to us. Pro keeps working until the paid period ends.

4. Delivery and use

After payment you receive a licence key on the confirmation page. Entering the key in OptionsCone downloads and installs Pro. One key can be used on up to two of your own computers. Pro checks the licence over the internet at least every five days; without a valid licence the Pro functions stop until the next successful check. The use of the software is governed by the licence terms shipped with OptionsCone (LICENSE file).

Privacy

Controller

, contact details in the legal notice above.

This website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). To deliver the pages, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data and may keep them in server logs. Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data; it counts page, referrer, screen size and country. Fonts are loaded from this website, not from third parties.

The app

OptionsCone downloads market data from your computer directly from its sources, such as Yahoo Finance; we receive none of it. Everything you download stays on your computer.

When you activate or renew Pro, the app sends your licence key and an anonymous machine identifier (a one-way hash, from which your computer cannot be identified) to our licence server, which runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc., USA). The server stores the key, the machine identifier and the dates of activation and last check, to limit a key to two computers. It processes your IP address only to answer the request.

Purchase

The payment is handled by Link / Stripe as merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. From Stripe we receive and store the status of your subscription, its end date and the email address you entered, linked to your licence key, to keep the licence valid and to answer support requests.

Legal basis, storage, your rights

The data is processed to perform the contract (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR) and, for the website, on the basis of our legitimate interest in a secure and working site (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Licence data is kept while the subscription runs and deleted when it is no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Some providers named above are in the USA; transfers are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Write to the email address in the legal notice.
